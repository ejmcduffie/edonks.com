Strategic Briefing

Where this goes

Electrifying big-wheel culture as a high-demand cultural wedge — scaling the modular E-Box platform across the broader classic American car and restomod market.

The Investment Thesis

The Wedge & The Horizon

Big-wheel culture is the ultimate proving ground for high-torque electric powertrains. The modular architecture we develop to conquer 26″ to 30″+ unsprung mass directly unlocks the entire classic American car aftermarket.

High Passion, High Spend

The Cultural Wedge

Donks, boxes, and bubbles command six-figure custom builds. Builders invest heavily in powertrains, suspension, and paint, yet traditional EV manufacturers ignore them entirely. By proving our powertrain in the culture's most demanding platform, EDONKS captures authentic cultural leadership and high-margin early adoption.

Universal Body-Mount Architecture

Classic Car Scale

While donks are the flagship showcase, the E-Box platform is designed for cross-platform scalability. From GM B-bodies (Caprice, Impala) and G-bodies (Cutlass, Regal, Monte Carlo) to classic C10 trucks and vintage American cruisers, our modular chassis accommodates universal body mounts, standardizing classic EV conversions.

Geometry, Braking & Battery Integration

Defensible Engineering Moat

Standard EV conversion kits fail on heavy classic platforms. Big wheels compound scrub radius, rotational inertia, and braking heat. EDONKS engineers the chassis as a unified system: custom structural pack trays that maintain OEM weight balance, heavy-duty suspension geometry, and dedicated thermal management.

Phased Execution

The Four-Stage Roadmap

  1. 01

    Now

    Conversions

    One car at a time, in-house. The E1 first, then rider builds. Every conversion is paid engineering: it produces parts, process, and documentation the platform needs.

    • —Full custom electric conversions for donks, boxes, and bubbles
    • —Documented builds — the log is the product too
    • —Direct rider relationships and street validation from day one
  2. 02

    Next

    Kits

    Package what repeats. Once a subsystem is proven across builds, it becomes a kit a qualified speed shop can order and install without bespoke re-engineering.

    • —Subsystem kits: high-torque drive, pack tray, big-wheel suspension & brake packages
    • —Comprehensive shop documentation and technical field support
    • —Certified installer network establishing regional build hubs
  3. 03

    The big one

    Licensed E-Box platform

    The full modular rolling chassis, licensed and manufactured at scale, with defined body-mount interfaces for donks and classic American restomods.

    • —Turnkey rolling chassis supplied directly to builders, restoration shops, and OEMs
    • —Published body-mount interface specs across GM B-body, G-body, and classic truck platforms
    • —Licensing partnerships to scale delivery well beyond our in-house shop capacity
  4. 04

    Alongside

    Events & media

    The brand lives at the shows and on the timeline. Build content, race demonstrations, and the Culture Archive as a permanent record — authentic cultural gravity.

    • —Show, drag strip, and festival presence, anchored in Florida and expanding nationwide
    • —Cinematic build documentary series and high-production long-form content
    • —The Culture Archive, immortalizing the lineage and the riders who built the scene

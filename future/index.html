What's next

Where this goes

Four stages. Each one funds and de-risks the next. No stage skips the one before it.

  1. 01

    Now

    Conversions

    One car at a time, in-house. The E1 first, then rider builds. Every conversion is paid engineering: it produces parts, process, and documentation the platform needs.

    • —Full custom electric conversions for donks, boxes, and bubbles
    • —Documented builds — the log is the product too
    • —Direct rider relationships from day one
  2. 02

    Next

    Kits

    Package what repeats. Once a subsystem is proven across builds, it becomes a kit a qualified shop can order and install without redesigning it.

    • —Subsystem kits: brakes, suspension, pack tray, drive
    • —Installation documentation and shop support
    • —Certified installer network, scope still being defined
  3. 03

    The big one

    Licensed E-Box platform

    The full modular chassis, licensed and produced at volume, with defined body-mount interfaces for the cars riders actually build.

    • —Rolling chassis supplied to builders and shops
    • —Published body-mount interface specs per body style
    • —Licensing to partners outside our own build capacity
  4. 04

    Alongside

    Events & media

    The brand lives at the shows and on the timeline. Build content, show presence, and the Culture Archive as a permanent record — not marketing bolted on afterward.

    • —Show and race presence, starting in Florida
    • —Build series and long-form documentation
    • —The Culture Archive, credited to the riders who fill it

Classic Soul. Electric Power.

EDONKS electrifies old-school big-wheel cars — donks, boxes, and bubbles — without erasing the culture that made them. Born in Florida and built by a rider, EDONKS started with the E1, founder EJ's own electric donk, and grew into the E-Box: a modular electric chassis platform purpose-built for the physics of big wheels.

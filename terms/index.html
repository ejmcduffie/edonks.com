What this site is

EDONKS is an early-stage project. The E1 is a prototype build in progress. The E-Box is a platform in development, not a product you can buy today. This site tells that story as it happens.

Specs are targets

Numbers on this site — range, power, weight, battery size, timelines, and the like — are design targets or estimates unless we clearly say they were measured. They can and will change as the engineering gets real. Don't rely on them for a purchase, a build plan, or a safety decision.

Renders are concept art

Renders, visualizations, and mockups are concept art. They show where the work is headed, not a finished vehicle that exists or is available. Unless an image is captioned as a photo of a specific real car, don't treat it as one.

No offer of sale

Nothing on this site is an offer to sell, a quote, or a contract. Prices, budgets, availability, and timelines mentioned here are not commitments. We don't take payments through this site.

Inquiries and reservations

Sending the Get Your Donk Electrified form or reserving an E-Box spot is non-binding on both sides. It tells us you're interested and lets us reach out — nothing more. No build, deposit, purchase, or delivery date exists unless we both sign a separate written agreement.

Not engineering advice

What we share about electric conversions is general information about our own work. Converting a car to electric means high-voltage systems, heavy batteries, and changes to brakes, suspension, and weight balance — real risks. Don't attempt that work based on this site; use qualified professionals.

EDONKS

Classic Soul. Electric Power.

EDONKS electrifies old-school big-wheel cars — donks, boxes, and bubbles — without erasing the culture that made them. Born in Florida and built by a rider, EDONKS started with the E1, founder EJ's own electric donk, and grew into the E-Box: a modular electric chassis platform purpose-built for the physics of big wheels.

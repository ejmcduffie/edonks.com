Eras

How the look moved decade to decade.

  • Era

    Late '80s–early '90s — South Florida origins

    The start: Miami, the Dirty South, and the first lifted Impalas on big rims.

  • Era

    Late '90s — the word travels

    Miami music carries the term and the look out past Florida.

  • Era

    2000s — show circuit era

    Bigger wheels, deeper candy, full show builds — and in 2006, the culture got its own magazine.

  • Era

    2010s — racing and craft

    NDRA weekends, swapped motors, and build quality as the new flex.

  • Era

    2020s — now

    Donk Day, Liberty City's MLK weekend rideout, and where electric fits into it.

Cars

The bodies that carry the culture.

  • Donk

    '71–'76 Impala / Caprice

    The original. The emblem that gave the whole thing its name.

  • Box

    '77–'90 Impala / Caprice

    Squared off, flat glass, endlessly built.

  • Bubble

    '91–'96 Caprice / Impala SS

    Rounded body, heavy presence.

  • Body-on-frame

    Crown Victoria

    Cheap, plentiful, and it sits right.

  • Body-on-frame

    Lincoln Town Car

    Long-body luxury on big wheels.

  • A-body / G-body

    Cutlass / Monte Carlo

    Two-door staples in the scene.

Wheel styles

20s to 30s and up. The wheel is half the car.

  • Style

    Directional multi-spoke

    The show-lot standard. Deep lips, tight tires.

  • Style

    Floaters

    Center stays upright while the wheel turns.

  • Style

    Wire wheels, big diameter

    Classic pattern scaled up.

  • Style

    Forged one-offs

    Custom-built for a single car.

  • Sizes

    26s / 28s / 30s+

    Where the physics — and the brake conversation — gets serious.

Events

Shows, races, and weekends that matter.

  • Racing

    NDRA racing

    Big wheels on the strip, real timeslips.

  • Signature event

    Donk Day

    MLK weekend in Liberty City. OG builds from the '90s parked next to new-school debuts — the scene's homecoming.

Submit your ride

What to send

  • —Year, make, and model — and whether it's a donk, box, bubble, or another hi-riser
  • —Photos, and who shot them
  • —The story: how you got into it, who put you on, the build, the shows
  • —Everyone to credit — painter, upholsterer, fabricator, the shop
  • —How you want to be credited: your name, a handle, or anonymous

Get Your Donk Electrified
EDONKS

Classic Soul. Electric Power.

EDONKS electrifies old-school big-wheel cars — donks, boxes, and bubbles — without erasing the culture that made them. Born in Florida and built by a rider, EDONKS started with the E1, founder EJ's own electric donk, and grew into the E-Box: a modular electric chassis platform purpose-built for the physics of big wheels.

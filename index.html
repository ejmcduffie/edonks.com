Classic Soul. Electric Power.

EDONKS electrifies old-school big-wheel cars — donks, boxes, and bubbles — without erasing the culture that made them. Born in Florida and built by a rider, EDONKS started with the E1, founder EJ's own electric donk, and grew into the E-Box: a modular electric chassis platform purpose-built for the physics of big wheels.

Design

Built different.

0102030405/ 05

  1. 26-inch gold wire wheels

    26-inch wire-spoke wheels on low-profile rubber — the donk stance, kept.

    Design point

  2. Quad headlamps. Gold mesh grille.

    Four round headlamps over a gold mesh grille — the face stays donk.

  3. Three electric motors

    One motor at the front axle, two at the rear — the concept drive layout for the E1.

    Concept spec

  4. 84.8 kWh pack, low and centered

    84.8 kWh gross from 16 Tesla Model S modules, 96s, 364.8 V nominal — set low and centered between the frame rails.

  5. Tan leather cabin

    Tan leather inside — classic cabin, modern power underneath.

Classic Soul. Electric Power.

EDONKS electrifies old-school big-wheel cars — donks, boxes, and bubbles — without erasing the culture that made them. Born in Florida and built by a rider, EDONKS started with the E1, founder EJ's own electric donk, and grew into the E-Box: a modular electric chassis platform purpose-built for the physics of big wheels.

Design

Built different.

0102030405/ 05

  1. Regenerative Braking

    Regen slows the rolling 26s and recharges the pack. 16-inch 6-piston calipers handle the hard stops.

    Tech Brief

  2. Dual-Loop Liquid Cooling

    Twin radiators chill the battery pack and dual inverters. Keeps full power running in Florida heat.

    Thermal System

  3. Dual Rear Motors

    Twin motors on the rear axle deliver 1,200 hp with instant torque vectoring. Keeps the front open for 26-inch steering clearance.

    1,200 HP RWD

  4. 800V Battery & HV Delivery

    An 800-volt pack slung low between frame rails lowers center of mass. Shielded conduits pulse direct high-voltage power to the motors.

    800V Architecture

  5. Concert Cabin & 800V Climate

    Tan leather cockpit packs a 5 kW DC-DC converter for competition audio with zero voltage sag, plus instant electric A/C.

    Concert Cabin
